Cost of Living in Manchester

Summary of cost of living in Manchester, United Kingdom:

  • The estimated monthly costs for a family of four are €3,381.7 (£2,859.6), excluding rent (using our estimator).
  • The estimated monthly costs for a single person are €952.7 (£805.6), excluding rent.
  • Manchester is 29.6% less expensive than Oslo (excluding rent, see our cost of living index).
  • Rent in Manchester is, on average, 9.5% lower than in Oslo.
