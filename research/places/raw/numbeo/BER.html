Cost of Living in Berlin

Summary of cost of living in Berlin, Germany:

  • The estimated monthly costs for a family of four are 38,837.3 kr (€3,622.7), excluding rent (using our estimator).
  • The estimated monthly costs for a single person are 11,309.1 kr (€1,054.9), excluding rent.
  • Berlin is 24.6% less expensive than Oslo (excluding rent, see our cost of living index).
  • Rent in Berlin is, on average, 17.1% lower than in Oslo.
