Cost of Living in Edinburgh

Summary of cost of living in Edinburgh, United Kingdom:

  • The estimated monthly costs for a family of four are €3,694.1 (£3,123.7), excluding rent (using our estimator).
  • The estimated monthly costs for a single person are €1,057.5 (£894.2), excluding rent.
  • Edinburgh is 22.7% less expensive than Oslo (excluding rent, see our cost of living index).
  • Rent in Edinburgh is, on average, 14.1% lower than in Oslo.
