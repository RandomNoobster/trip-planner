The average cost of living in Lyon is $1747, which is in the top 33% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 3024th out of 9294 in our global list, 43rd out of 151 in France, and 3rd out of 14 in Auvergne-Rhone-Alpes.

The median after-tax salary is $3258, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.9 months. Ranked 234th (TOP 3%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 5th best city to live in France, and 1st most liveable city in Auvergne-Rhone-Alpes. With an estimated population of 519K, Lyon is the 3rd largest city in France.
