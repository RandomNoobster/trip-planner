The average cost of living in Palma de Mallorca is $2017, which is in the top 21% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 1973rd out of 9294 in our global list and 6th out of 153 in Spain.

The median after-tax salary is $2114, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1 months. Ranked 830th (TOP 9%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 30th best city to live in Spain. With an estimated population of 435K, Palma de Mallorca is the 8th largest city in Spain.
