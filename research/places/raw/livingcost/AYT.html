The average cost of living in Antalya is $994, which is in the top 40% of the least expensive cities in the world, ranked 5620th out of 9294 in our global list and 5th out of 128 in Turkey.

The median after-tax salary is $952, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1 months. Ranked 4797th (TOP 52%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 11th best city to live in Turkey. With an estimated population of 2.43M, Antalya is the 5th largest city in Turkey.
