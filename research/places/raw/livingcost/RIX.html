The average cost of living in Riga is $1254, which is close to the world's average cost of living, ranked 4631st out of 9294 in our global list and 2nd out of 10 in Latvia.

The median after-tax salary is $1555, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.2 months. Ranked 657th (TOP 7%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 1st best city to live in Latvia. With an estimated population of 595K, Riga is the 1st largest city in Latvia.
