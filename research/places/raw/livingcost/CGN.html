The average cost of living in Cologne is $1941, which is in the top 24% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 2200th out of 9294 in our global list, 19th out of 662 in Germany, and 3rd out of 201 in North Rhine-Westphalia.

The median after-tax salary is $3341, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.7 months. Ranked 21st (TOP 0.2%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 2nd best city to live in Germany, and 2nd most liveable city in North Rhine-Westphalia. With an estimated population of 1.09M, Cologne is the 4th largest city in Germany.
