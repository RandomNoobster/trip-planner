The average cost of living in Dublin is $3020, which is in the top 2% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 214th out of 9294 in our global list and 1st out of 12 in Ireland.

The median after-tax salary is $3998, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.3 months. Ranked 42nd (TOP 0.5%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 1st best city to live in Ireland. With an estimated population of 593K, Dublin is the 1st largest city in Ireland.
