The average cost of living in Nice is $1901, which is in the top 25% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 2367th out of 9294 in our global list, 20th out of 151 in France, and 2nd out of 10 in Provence-Alpes-Cote d'Azur.

The median after-tax salary is $2737, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.4 months. Ranked 612th (TOP 7%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 23rd best city to live in France, and 3rd most liveable city in Provence-Alpes-Cote d'Azur. With an estimated population of 358K, Nice is the 5th largest city in France.
