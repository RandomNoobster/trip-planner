The average cost of living in Tbilisi is $1053, which is close to the world's average cost of living, ranked 5182nd out of 9294 in our global list and 1st out of 10 in Georgia.

The median after-tax salary is $654, which is enough to cover living expenses for 0.6 months. Ranked 3429th (TOP 37%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 1st best city to live in Georgia. With an estimated population of 1.12M, Tbilisi is the 1st largest city in Georgia.
