The average cost of living in Rovaniemi is $1486, which is close to the world's average cost of living, ranked 4036th out of 9294 in our global list and 28th out of 49 in Finland.

The median after-tax salary is $3908, which is enough to cover living expenses for 2.6 months. Ranked 346th (TOP 4%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 7th best city to live in Finland. With an estimated population of 65.3K, Rovaniemi is the 17th largest city in Finland.
