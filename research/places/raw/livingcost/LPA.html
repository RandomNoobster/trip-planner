The average cost of living in Las Palmas is $1508, which is close to the world's average cost of living, ranked 3959th out of 9294 in our global list and 55th out of 153 in Spain.

The median after-tax salary is $1766, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.2 months. Ranked 684th (TOP 7%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 22nd best city to live in Spain. With an estimated population of 382K, Las Palmas is the 9th largest city in Spain.
