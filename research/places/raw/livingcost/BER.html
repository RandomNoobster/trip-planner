The average cost of living in Berlin is $2371, which is in the top 12% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 1150th out of 9294 in our global list, 2nd out of 662 in Germany, and 1st out of 24 in Brandenburg.

The median after-tax salary is $3423, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.4 months. Ranked 45th (TOP 0.5%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 9th best city to live in Germany, and 1st most liveable city in Brandenburg. With an estimated population of 3.78M, Berlin is the 1st largest city in Germany.
