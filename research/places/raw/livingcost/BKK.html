The average cost of living in Bangkok is $996, which is in the top 40% of the least expensive cities in the world, ranked 5606th out of 9294 in our global list and 2nd out of 35 in Thailand.

The median after-tax salary is $635, which is enough to cover living expenses for 0.6 months. Ranked 2044th (TOP 22%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 1st best city to live in Thailand. With an estimated population of 5.68M, Bangkok is the 1st largest city in Thailand.
