The average cost of living in Hamburg City is $2189, which is in the top 16% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 1511th out of 9294 in our global list, 4th out of 662 in Germany, and 1st out of 1 in Hamburg State.

The median after-tax salary is $3748, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.7 months. Ranked 38th (TOP 0.4%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 6th best city to live in Germany, and 1st most liveable city in Hamburg State. With an estimated population of 1.91M, Hamburg City is the 2nd largest city in Germany.
