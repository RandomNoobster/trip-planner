The average cost of living in Malaga is $1705, which is in the top 34% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 3191st out of 9294 in our global list and 19th out of 153 in Spain.

The median after-tax salary is $1960, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.1 months. Ranked 690th (TOP 7%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 25th best city to live in Spain. With an estimated population of 599K, Malaga is the 7th largest city in Spain.
