The average cost of living in Belgrade is $1349, which is close to the world's average cost of living, ranked 4429th out of 9294 in our global list and 1st out of 10 in Serbia.

The median after-tax salary is $1238, which is enough to cover living expenses for 0.9 months. Ranked 2274th (TOP 24%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 1st best city to live in Serbia. With an estimated population of 1.2M, Belgrade is the 1st largest city in Serbia.
