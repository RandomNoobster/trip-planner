The cost of living in the Netherlands is $2377, which is 2.05 times more expensive than the world average. The Netherlands ranked 13th out of 197 countries by cost of living and the 8th best country to live in.

The average salary after taxes in the Netherlands is $3890, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.6 months.
