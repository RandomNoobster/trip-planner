The cost of living in the Czech Republic is $1478, which is 1.27 times more expensive than the world average. The Czech Republic ranked 40th out of 197 countries by cost of living and the 32nd best country to live in.

The average salary after taxes in the Czech Republic is $1859, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.3 months.
