The average cost of living in Paris is $2460, which is in the top 10% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 964th out of 9294 in our global list, 1st out of 151 in France, and 1st out of 27 in Ile-de-France.

The median after-tax salary is $3694, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.5 months. Ranked 34th (TOP 0.4%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 1st best city to live in France, and 1st most liveable city in Ile-de-France. With an estimated population of 2.1M, Paris is the 1st largest city in France.
