The average cost of living in Zagreb is $1487, which is close to the world's average cost of living, ranked 4035th out of 9294 in our global list and 7th out of 15 in Croatia.

The median after-tax salary is $1896, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.3 months. Ranked 835th (TOP 9%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 1st best city to live in Croatia. With an estimated population of 767K, Zagreb is the 1st largest city in Croatia.
