The average cost of living in Rome is $1972, which is in the top 23% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 2104th out of 9294 in our global list, 2nd out of 148 in Italy, and 1st out of 12 in Lazio.

The median after-tax salary is $2041, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1 months. Ranked 134th (TOP 1%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 1st best city to live in Italy, and 1st most liveable city in Lazio. With an estimated population of 2.75M, Rome is the 1st largest city in Italy.
