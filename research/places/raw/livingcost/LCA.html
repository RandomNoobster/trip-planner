The average cost of living in Larnaca is $1440, which is close to the world's average cost of living, ranked 4181st out of 9294 in our global list and 5th out of 10 in Cyprus.

The median after-tax salary is $1975, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.4 months. Ranked 459th (TOP 5%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 1st best city to live in Cyprus. With an estimated population of 59.2K, Larnaca is the 2nd largest city in Cyprus.
