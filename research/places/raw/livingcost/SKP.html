The average cost of living in Skopje is $880, which is in the top 31% of the least expensive cities in the world, ranked 6377th out of 9294 in our global list and 1st out of 10 in North Macedonia.

The median after-tax salary is $896, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1 months. Ranked 4420th (TOP 48%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 1st best city to live in North Macedonia. With an estimated population of 423K, Skopje is the 1st largest city in North Macedonia.
