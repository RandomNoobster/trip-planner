The average cost of living in Turin is $1659, which is in the top 36% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 3356th out of 9294 in our global list, 24th out of 148 in Italy, and 1st out of 9 in Piedmont.

The median after-tax salary is $2078, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.3 months. Ranked 464th (TOP 5%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 8th best city to live in Italy, and 2nd most liveable city in Piedmont. With an estimated population of 842K, Turin is the 4th largest city in Italy.
