The average cost of living in Palermo is $1176, which is close to the world's average cost of living, ranked 4806th out of 9294 in our global list, 138th out of 148 in Italy, and 6th out of 10 in Sicily.

The median after-tax salary is $1558, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.3 months. Ranked 5211th (TOP 56%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 113th best city to live in Italy, and 4th most liveable city in Sicily. With an estimated population of 630K, Palermo is the 5th largest city in Italy.
