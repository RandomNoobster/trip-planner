The average cost of living in Gothenburg is $1879, which is in the top 27% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 2470th out of 9294 in our global list and 4th out of 60 in Sweden.

The median after-tax salary is $3784, which is enough to cover living expenses for 2 months. Ranked 91st (TOP 1%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 2nd best city to live in Sweden. With an estimated population of 675K, Gothenburg is the 2nd largest city in Sweden.
