The average cost of living in Bratislava is $1590, which is close to the world's average cost of living, ranked 3630th out of 9294 in our global list and 1st out of 23 in Slovakia.

The median after-tax salary is $1723, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.1 months. Ranked 662nd (TOP 7%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 1st best city to live in Slovakia. With an estimated population of 481K, Bratislava is the 1st largest city in Slovakia.
