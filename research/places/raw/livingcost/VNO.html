The average cost of living in Vilnius is $1434, which is close to the world's average cost of living, ranked 4201st out of 9294 in our global list and 1st out of 10 in Lithuania.

The median after-tax salary is $1987, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.4 months. Ranked 502nd (TOP 5%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 1st best city to live in Lithuania. With an estimated population of 581K, Vilnius is the 1st largest city in Lithuania.
