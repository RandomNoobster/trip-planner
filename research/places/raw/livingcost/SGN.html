The average cost of living in Ho Chi Minh is $726, which is in the top 23% of the least expensive cities in the world, ranked 7123rd out of 9294 in our global list and 1st out of 38 in Vietnam.

The median after-tax salary is $500, which is enough to cover living expenses for 0.7 months. Ranked 3396th (TOP 37%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 1st best city to live in Vietnam. With an estimated population of 14M, Ho Chi Minh is the 1st largest city in Vietnam.
