The average cost of living in Prague is $1899, which is in the top 26% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 2377th out of 9294 in our global list and 1st out of 58 in the Czech Republic.

The median after-tax salary is $2366, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.2 months. Ranked 244th (TOP 3%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 1st best city to live in the Czech Republic. With an estimated population of 1.4M, Prague is the 1st largest city in the Czech Republic.
