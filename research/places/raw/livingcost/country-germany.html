The cost of living in Germany is $1764, which is 1.52 times more expensive than the world average. Germany ranked 26th out of 197 countries by cost of living and the 12th best country to live in.

The average salary after taxes in Germany is $3363, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.9 months.
