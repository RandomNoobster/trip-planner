The average cost of living in London is $3662, which is in the top 0.3% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 28th out of 9294 in our global list, 1st out of 277 in the United Kingdom, and 1st out of 231 in England.

The median after-tax salary is $4720, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.3 months. Ranked 4th (TOP 0.1%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 1st best city to live in the United Kingdom, and 1st most liveable city in England. With an estimated population of 8.8M, London is the 1st largest city in the United Kingdom.
