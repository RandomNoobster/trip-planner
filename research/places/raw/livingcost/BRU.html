The average cost of living in Brussels is $2030, which is in the top 21% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 1939th out of 9294 in our global list and 1st out of 91 in Belgium.

The median after-tax salary is $3313, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.6 months. Ranked 121st (TOP 1%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 1st best city to live in Belgium. With an estimated population of 196K, Brussels is the 4th largest city in Belgium.
