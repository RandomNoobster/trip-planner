The average cost of living in Seoul is $1296, which is close to the world's average cost of living, ranked 4545th out of 9294 in our global list and 3rd out of 87 in South Korea.

The median after-tax salary is $3066, which is enough to cover living expenses for 2.4 months. Ranked 16th (TOP 0.2%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 1st best city to live in South Korea. With an estimated population of 9.67M, Seoul is the 1st largest city in South Korea.
