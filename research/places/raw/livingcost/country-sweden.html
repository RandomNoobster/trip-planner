The cost of living in Sweden is $1758, which is 1.51 times more expensive than the world average. Sweden ranked 27th out of 197 countries by cost of living and the 9th best country to live in.

The average salary after taxes in Sweden is $3340, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.9 months.
