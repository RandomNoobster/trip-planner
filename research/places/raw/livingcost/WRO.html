The average cost of living in Wroclaw is $1576, which is close to the world's average cost of living, ranked 3695th out of 9294 in our global list and 5th out of 79 in Poland.

The median after-tax salary is $1896, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.2 months. Ranked 527th (TOP 6%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 4th best city to live in Poland. With an estimated population of 674K, Wroclaw is the 3rd largest city in Poland.
