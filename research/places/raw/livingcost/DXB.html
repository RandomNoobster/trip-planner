The average cost of living in Dubai is $2470, which is in the top 10% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 947th out of 9294 in our global list and 1st out of 9 in the United Arab Emirates.

The median after-tax salary is $4153, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.7 months. Ranked 184th (TOP 2%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 1st best city to live in the United Arab Emirates. With an estimated population of 3.94M, Dubai is the 1st largest city in the United Arab Emirates.
