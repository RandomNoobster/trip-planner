The average cost of living in Athens is $1439, which is close to the world's average cost of living, ranked 4184th out of 9294 in our global list and 5th out of 50 in Greece.

The median after-tax salary is $1330, which is enough to cover living expenses for 0.9 months. Ranked 602nd (TOP 6%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 1st best city to live in Greece. With an estimated population of 643K, Athens is the 1st largest city in Greece.
