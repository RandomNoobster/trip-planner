The average cost of living in Barcelona is $2129, which is in the top 18% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 1659th out of 9294 in our global list and 3rd out of 153 in Spain.

The median after-tax salary is $2324, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.1 months. Ranked 174th (TOP 2%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 2nd best city to live in Spain. With an estimated population of 1.73M, Barcelona is the 2nd largest city in Spain.
