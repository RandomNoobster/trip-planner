The average cost of living in Dubrovnik is $1732, which is in the top 33% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 3083rd out of 9294 in our global list and 1st out of 15 in Croatia.

The median after-tax salary is $1530, which is enough to cover living expenses for 0.9 months. Ranked 3488th (TOP 38%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 7th best city to live in Croatia. With an estimated population of 41.6K, Dubrovnik is the 11th largest city in Croatia.
