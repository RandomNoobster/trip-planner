The average cost of living in Seville is $1343, which is close to the world's average cost of living, ranked 4438th out of 9294 in our global list and 92nd out of 153 in Spain.

The median after-tax salary is $1797, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.3 months. Ranked 457th (TOP 5%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 9th best city to live in Spain. With an estimated population of 689K, Seville is the 6th largest city in Spain.
