The average cost of living in Lisbon is $1966, which is in the top 23% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 2123rd out of 9294 in our global list and 1st out of 124 in Portugal.

The median after-tax salary is $1605, which is enough to cover living expenses for 0.8 months. Ranked 532nd (TOP 6%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 1st best city to live in Portugal. With an estimated population of 546K, Lisbon is the 1st largest city in Portugal.
