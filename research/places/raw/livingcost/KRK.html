The average cost of living in Krakow is $1636, which is in the top 37% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 3457th out of 9294 in our global list and 3rd out of 79 in Poland.

The median after-tax salary is $1969, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.2 months. Ranked 497th (TOP 5%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 2nd best city to live in Poland. With an estimated population of 804K, Krakow is the 2nd largest city in Poland.
