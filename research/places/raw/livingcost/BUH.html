The average cost of living in Bucharest is $1148, which is close to the world's average cost of living, ranked 4877th out of 9294 in our global list and 2nd out of 47 in Romania.

The median after-tax salary is $1455, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.3 months. Ranked 482nd (TOP 5%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 1st best city to live in Romania. With an estimated population of 1.72M, Bucharest is the 1st largest city in Romania.
