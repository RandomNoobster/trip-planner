The average cost of living in Yerevan is $1205, which is close to the world's average cost of living, ranked 4743rd out of 9294 in our global list and 1st out of 10 in Armenia.

The median after-tax salary is $669, which is enough to cover living expenses for 0.6 months. Ranked 4225th (TOP 45%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 1st best city to live in Armenia. With an estimated population of 1.11M, Yerevan is the 1st largest city in Armenia.
