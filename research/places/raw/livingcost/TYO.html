The average cost of living in Tokyo is $1679, which is in the top 35% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 3273rd out of 9294 in our global list and 3rd out of 907 in Japan.

The median after-tax salary is $2429, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.4 months. Ranked 9th (TOP 0.1%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 1st best city to live in Japan. With an estimated population of 14.3M, Tokyo is the 1st largest city in Japan.
