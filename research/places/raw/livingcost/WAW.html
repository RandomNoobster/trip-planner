The average cost of living in Warsaw is $1979, which is in the top 22% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 2081st out of 9294 in our global list and 1st out of 79 in Poland.

The median after-tax salary is $2218, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.1 months. Ranked 335th (TOP 4%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 1st best city to live in Poland. With an estimated population of 1.86M, Warsaw is the 1st largest city in Poland.
