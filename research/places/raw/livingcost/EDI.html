The average cost of living in Edinburgh is $2295, which is in the top 14% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 1307th out of 9294 in our global list, 38th out of 277 in the United Kingdom, and 1st out of 21 in Scotland.

The median after-tax salary is $3514, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.5 months. Ranked 83rd (TOP 0.9%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 11th best city to live in the United Kingdom, and 2nd most liveable city in Scotland. With an estimated population of 488K, Edinburgh is the 8th largest city in the United Kingdom.
