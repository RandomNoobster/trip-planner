The average cost of living in Tallinn is $1594, which is in the top 39% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 3619th out of 9294 in our global list and 1st out of 10 in Estonia.

The median after-tax salary is $2188, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.4 months. Ranked 357th (TOP 4%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 1st best city to live in Estonia. With an estimated population of 458K, Tallinn is the 1st largest city in Estonia.
