The average cost of living in Valletta is $1824, which is in the top 29% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 2712th out of 9294 in our global list and 3rd out of 10 in Malta.

The median after-tax salary is $2178, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.2 months. Ranked 1347th (TOP 14%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 7th best city to live in Malta. With an estimated population of 6.44K, Valletta is the 10th largest city in Malta.
