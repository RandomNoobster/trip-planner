The average cost of living in Florence is $1891, which is in the top 26% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 2424th out of 9294 in our global list, 4th out of 148 in Italy, and 1st out of 10 in Tuscany.

The median after-tax salary is $2073, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.1 months. Ranked 478th (TOP 5%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 13th best city to live in Italy, and 1st most liveable city in Tuscany. With an estimated population of 361K, Florence is the 8th largest city in Italy.
