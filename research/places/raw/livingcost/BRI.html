The average cost of living in Bari is $1617, which is in the top 38% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 3520th out of 9294 in our global list, 32nd out of 148 in Italy, and 1st out of 11 in Apulia.

The median after-tax salary is $1962, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.2 months.
