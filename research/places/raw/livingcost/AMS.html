The average cost of living in Amsterdam is $3165, which is in the top 1% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 138th out of 9294 in our global list and 1st out of 70 in the Netherlands.

The median after-tax salary is $4747, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.5 months. Ranked 24th (TOP 0.3%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 3rd best city to live in the Netherlands. With an estimated population of 921K, Amsterdam is the 1st largest city in the Netherlands.
