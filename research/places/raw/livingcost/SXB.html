The average cost of living in Strasbourg is $1658, which is in the top 36% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 3362nd out of 9294 in our global list, 56th out of 151 in France, and 1st out of 12 in Grand Est.

The median after-tax salary is $2941, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.8 months. Ranked 462nd (TOP 5%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 14th best city to live in France, and 1st most liveable city in Grand Est. With an estimated population of 294K, Strasbourg is the 8th largest city in France.
