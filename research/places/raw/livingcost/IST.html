The average cost of living in Istanbul is $1328, which is close to the world's average cost of living, ranked 4475th out of 9294 in our global list and 1st out of 128 in Turkey.

The median after-tax salary is $1177, which is enough to cover living expenses for 0.9 months. Ranked 829th (TOP 9%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 1st best city to live in Turkey. With an estimated population of 15.7M, Istanbul is the 1st largest city in Turkey.
