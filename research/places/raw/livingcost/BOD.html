The average cost of living in Bordeaux is $1789, which is in the top 31% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 2862nd out of 9294 in our global list, 36th out of 151 in France, and 1st out of 10 in Nouvelle-Aquitaine.

The median after-tax salary is $3074, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.7 months. Ranked 442nd (TOP 5%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 11th best city to live in France, and 1st most liveable city in Nouvelle-Aquitaine. With an estimated population of 268K, Bordeaux is the 9th largest city in France.
