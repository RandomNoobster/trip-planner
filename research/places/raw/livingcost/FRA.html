The average cost of living in Frankfurt is $2251, which is in the top 15% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 1384th out of 9294 in our global list, 3rd out of 662 in Germany, and 1st out of 57 in Hesse.

The median after-tax salary is $4308, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.9 months. Ranked 48th (TOP 0.5%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 10th best city to live in Germany, and 1st most liveable city in Hesse. With an estimated population of 776K, Frankfurt is the 5th largest city in Germany.
