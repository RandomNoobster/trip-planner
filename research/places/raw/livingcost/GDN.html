The average cost of living in Gdansk is $1607, which is in the top 38% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 3554th out of 9294 in our global list and 4th out of 79 in Poland.

The median after-tax salary is $1939, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.2 months. Ranked 671st (TOP 7%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 8th best city to live in Poland. With an estimated population of 488K, Gdansk is the 6th largest city in Poland.
