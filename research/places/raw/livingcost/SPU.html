The average cost of living in Split is $1602, which is in the top 38% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 3578th out of 9294 in our global list and 3rd out of 15 in Croatia.

The median after-tax salary is $1648, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1 months. Ranked 1916th (TOP 21%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 4th best city to live in Croatia. With an estimated population of 161K, Split is the 2nd largest city in Croatia.
