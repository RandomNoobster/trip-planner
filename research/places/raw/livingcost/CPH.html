The average cost of living in Copenhagen is $2555, which is in the top 9% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 805th out of 9294 in our global list and 1st out of 27 in Denmark.

The median after-tax salary is $4784, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.9 months. Ranked 5th (TOP 0.1%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 1st best city to live in Denmark. With an estimated population of 667K, Copenhagen is the 1st largest city in Denmark.
