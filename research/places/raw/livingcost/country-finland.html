The cost of living in Finland is $1644, which is 1.41 times more expensive than the world average. Finland ranked 31st out of 197 countries by cost of living and the 11th best country to live in.

The average salary after taxes in Finland is $3033, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.8 months.
