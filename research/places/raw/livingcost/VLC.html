The average cost of living in Valencia is $1812, which is in the top 30% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 2757th out of 9294 in our global list and 9th out of 153 in Spain.

The median after-tax salary is $1958, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.1 months. Ranked 354th (TOP 4%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 6th best city to live in Spain. With an estimated population of 841K, Valencia is the 4th largest city in Spain.
