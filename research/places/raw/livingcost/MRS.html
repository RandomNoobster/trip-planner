The average cost of living in Marseille is $1759, which is in the top 32% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 2979th out of 9294 in our global list, 40th out of 151 in France, and 6th out of 10 in Provence-Alpes-Cote d'Azur.

The median after-tax salary is $2494, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.4 months. Ranked 453rd (TOP 5%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 12th best city to live in France, and 1st most liveable city in Provence-Alpes-Cote d'Azur. With an estimated population of 886K, Marseille is the 2nd largest city in France.
