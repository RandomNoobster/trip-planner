The average cost of living in Ljubljana is $1719, which is in the top 34% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 3144th out of 9294 in our global list and 1st out of 10 in Slovenia.

The median after-tax salary is $2014, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.2 months. Ranked 653rd (TOP 7%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 1st best city to live in Slovenia. With an estimated population of 284K, Ljubljana is the 1st largest city in Slovenia.
