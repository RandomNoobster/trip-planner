The average cost of living in Dresden is $1448, which is close to the world's average cost of living, ranked 4158th out of 9294 in our global list, 592nd out of 662 in Germany, and 12th out of 26 in Saxony.

The median after-tax salary is $3219, which is enough to cover living expenses for 2.2 months. Ranked 96th (TOP 1%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 17th best city to live in Germany, and 1st most liveable city in Saxony. With an estimated population of 566K, Dresden is the 12th largest city in Germany.
