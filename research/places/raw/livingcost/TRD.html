The average cost of living in Trondheim is $2470, which is in the top 10% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 942nd out of 9294 in our global list and 4th out of 33 in Norway.

The median after-tax salary is $4212, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.7 months. Ranked 390th (TOP 4%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 6th best city to live in Norway. With an estimated population of 199K, Trondheim is the 3rd largest city in Norway.
