The average cost of living in Salzburg is $2003, which is in the top 22% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 2011th out of 9294 in our global list and 4th out of 26 in Austria.

The median after-tax salary is $3378, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.7 months. Ranked 279th (TOP 3%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 3rd best city to live in Austria. With an estimated population of 155K, Salzburg is the 4th largest city in Austria.
