The average cost of living in Basel is $3034, which is in the top 2% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 201st out of 9294 in our global list and 35th out of 50 in Switzerland.

The median after-tax salary is $7958, which is enough to cover living expenses for 2.6 months. Ranked 29th (TOP 0.3%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 4th best city to live in Switzerland. With an estimated population of 176K, Basel is the 3rd largest city in Switzerland.
