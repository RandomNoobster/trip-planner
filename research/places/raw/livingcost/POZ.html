The average cost of living in Poznan is $1430, which is close to the world's average cost of living, ranked 4210th out of 9294 in our global list and 9th out of 79 in Poland.

The median after-tax salary is $1882, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.3 months. Ranked 522nd (TOP 6%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 3rd best city to live in Poland. With an estimated population of 547K, Poznan is the 5th largest city in Poland.
