The average cost of living in Naples is $1570, which is close to the world's average cost of living, ranked 3728th out of 9294 in our global list, 44th out of 148 in Italy, and 2nd out of 11 in Campania.

The median after-tax salary is $1889, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.2 months. Ranked 421st (TOP 5%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 5th best city to live in Italy, and 1st most liveable city in Campania. With an estimated population of 913K, Naples is the 3rd largest city in Italy.
