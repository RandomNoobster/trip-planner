The average cost of living in Singapore City is $3105, which is in the top 2% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 165th out of 9294 in our global list and 1st out of 2 in Singapore.

The median after-tax salary is $4276, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.4 months. Ranked 8th (TOP 0.1%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 1st best city to live in Singapore. With an estimated population of 5.87M, Singapore City is the 1st largest city in Singapore.
