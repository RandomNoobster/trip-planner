The average cost of living in Madrid is $2109, which is in the top 19% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 1723rd out of 9294 in our global list and 4th out of 153 in Spain.

The median after-tax salary is $2569, which is enough to cover living expenses for 1.2 months. Ranked 160th (TOP 2%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 1st best city to live in Spain. With an estimated population of 3.51M, Madrid is the 1st largest city in Spain.
