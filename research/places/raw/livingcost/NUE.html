The average cost of living in Nuremberg is $1753, which is in the top 32% of the most expensive cities in the world, ranked 2992nd out of 9294 in our global list, 152nd out of 662 in Germany, and 24th out of 68 in Bavaria.

The median after-tax salary is $3888, which is enough to cover living expenses for 2.2 months. Ranked 131st (TOP 1%) in the list of best places to live in the world and 25th best city to live in Germany, and 2nd most liveable city in Bavaria. With an estimated population of 526K, Nuremberg is the 14th largest city in Germany.
